Self-similar dimension

Box-counting estimate

Cover the shape with grids of shrinking box size ε and count the boxes it touches. Plotted on log scales the counts fall on a line, and its slope is the dimension. This works for rough shapes that have no exact copies of themselves, which is most fractals in nature.
