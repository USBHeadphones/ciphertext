Use it on other tabs

A web page can't reach into other tabs, so the clicker travels as a bookmarklet: the same engine and your current settings, packed into a bookmark.

⚡ Autoclicker

  1. Drag the red button onto your bookmarks bar (show it with Ctrl/⌘+Shift+B).
  2. On any site, click the bookmark. A small status badge appears in the corner.
  3. Use your hotkeys there. Click the bookmark again to remove it.

Change any setting here and re-drag the bookmark to update it.

Limits worth knowing

  • Browsers mark these clicks as synthetic (isTrusted = false). Most pages accept them, but some games and anti-bot checks ignore them. Only an OS-level tool (AutoHotkey, xdotool) gets past that.
  • Clicks stay inside the page. They can't reach the browser UI, other apps, or cross-origin iframes (ads, embedded players).
  • A few sites with strict security policies block bookmarklets.
  • Turbo works in short bursts and yields between them, so your keys and the panic stop keep working at full speed.
