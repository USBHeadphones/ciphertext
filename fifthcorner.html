THE FIFTH CORNER

THREE SPACES · ONE GYROSCOPE

You carry a gyroscope. It never turns unless you do: it is carried straight, which geometers call parallel transport. In flat space that makes it a perfect compass. In curved space it is not. Walk a loop and it comes back turned, by exactly the area the loop went around.

The doors in these spaces are gyro locks. Stand in front of one: if the needle points through it, it opens. Getting the needle to point the right way means walking the right loop.

THREE SPACES, ONE GYROSCOPE

The same instrument, carried around a loop in each space:

Flat space never turns it. Hyperbolic space turns it one way and spherical space the other, in each case by the area the loop went around. Curvature is not something you see. It is something you carry home and find has turned.
